fix(mobile): replace all ResponsiveContainer with explicit pixel dimensions

Charts not rendering in Capacitor Android WebView because ResizeObserver
fires with width=0. Fix: replaced every ResponsiveContainer usage in
WealthChart, WealthHistoryChart, and WhatIfView with a useRef + useLayoutEffect
pattern that measures real offsetWidth. Three setTimeout fallback polls
(100ms, 400ms, 800ms or 900ms) ensure the measurement fires even if the
WebView layout completes asynchronously. Charts now only render once
chartWidth > 0, preventing zero-size SVG rendering.
